Summoned from the sea off Angel Grove by a flute that doubles as a blade. Scroll.

01 — Command

Played,notpiloted

The Dragon Dagger is an enchanted flute as well as a weapon. Tommy Oliver usually commanded the Dragonzord remotely from the sidelines, taking its cockpit only when a fight demanded closer control.

02 — Armament

Fingermissiles.Taildrill.

Missiles launch from its fingertips, and the drill on the tip of its tail swings around to strike. Built for water, it fought submerged far better than the Dino Megazord.

03 — Allegiance

Whoeverholdsthecoin

The Zord answers to the Green Power Coin and the Dagger, not to a side. Rita Repulsa gave both to her evil Green Ranger; when the spell broke, Dragonzord followed Tommy to the Rangers — and when the Dagger was stolen, it turned on Angel Grove.
